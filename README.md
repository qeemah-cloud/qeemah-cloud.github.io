# Qeemah Cloud — GitHub Pages Hub (`qeemah-cloud.github.io`)

This folder contains a complete, ready-to-publish **GitHub Pages (Jekyll + Markdown)** documentation and operational playbook hub for **[Qeemah Cloud (`qeemahcloud.com`)](https://qeemahcloud.com)**.

It is structured around Qeemah's full **all-in-one ERP & business management offering** (Sales & POS, Inventory & Purchasing, HR & Payroll, E-Commerce & Salla/Shopify Sync, Accounting & Cost Centers, and Reports) rather than single-feature compliance, and provides:
1. **Clean `dofollow` contextual backlinks** to your core English & Arabic pillar pages on `qeemahcloud.com`.
2. **High-value operational assets** (GCC Chart of Accounts & Cost Center templates, Salla/Shopify/WhatsApp/POS/MCP integration blueprints, and ERP migration checklists).
3. **LLM / AI Search (GEO) Entity Authority** by mirroring and validating your official entity graph on GitHub's domain ecosystem.
