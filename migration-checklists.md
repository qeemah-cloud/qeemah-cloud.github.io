## 1. Pre-Migration Data Checklist (What We Move For You)

Whether you are moving from **Excel spreadsheets**, **Daftra (`دفترة`)**, **Qoyod (`قيود`)**, **Rewaa (`رواء`)**, **Wafeq (`وافق`)**, **Zoho Books**, or **Odoo**, here is the standard data cutover checklist our team handles:

### A. Chart of Accounts & Opening Trial Balance
- [ ] Export your current **Chart of Accounts (`شجرة الحسابات`)** and **Trial Balance (`ميزان المراجعة`)** as of your cutover date.
- [ ] Map active bank accounts (e.g., Al Rajhi, SNB, Riyad Bank, Alinma, SAB, Emirates NBD) and POS cash drawers.
- [ ] Define **Cost Centers (`مراكز التكلفة`)** for branches, projects, contracts, or equipment so historical and future P&L can be segmented cleanly.
  * *Need a cleaner structure?* See our [GCC Chart of Accounts & Cost Center Templates](./chart-of-accounts-templates.html).

### B. Product Catalog, Barcodes & Multi-Warehouse Inventory
- [ ] Export products with **Arabic & English names**, SKUs, and 1D/2D barcodes (including weighted-scale barcode prefixes if applicable).
- [ ] Configure **Units of Measure (UoM)** conversions (e.g., `1 Carton = 24 Pieces`, `1 Box = 6 Packs`) and pricing tiers (Retail, Wholesale, Custom).
- [ ] Import **opening stock quantities and unit costs** per warehouse/branch, including batch numbers and expiry dates for F&B, pharmacy, or wholesale items.
  * *Learn more:* [Qeemah Inventory & Warehouse Software](https://qeemahcloud.com/en/inventory-software-saudi/)

### C. Customers, Suppliers & Open AR/AP Aging
- [ ] Export customer and supplier master lists (Name, VAT/TRN number, National Address, phone/WhatsApp number, credit limit, payment terms).
- [ ] Import **unpaid / partially paid invoices** so your **AR Aging (`أعمار الديون`: 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days)** and customer statements are accurate from Day 1.
- [ ] Enable [Automated WhatsApp Payment Reminders](https://qeemahcloud.com/en/features/whatsapp-invoice-reminders/) for overdue receivables if desired.

### D. Employees, Attendance & Payroll Setup
- [ ] Import employee profiles, basic salaries, housing/transport allowances, GOSI status (Saudi / Non-Saudi), and IBAN details for WPS/Mudad exports.
- [ ] Set up branch clock-in kiosks and shift schedules.
