## How One Sale Connects Every Department

In [Qeemah Cloud](https://qeemahcloud.com/en/), a transaction is entered once and every team works from the same live record:

```
[Customer Order / POS Sale / Salla & Shopify Order]
        │
        ├──► 1. Inventory: Allocates or deducts stock in the fulfilling warehouse (with UoM & batch/expiry tracking)
        ├──► 2. Fulfillment: Moves through Draft → Confirmed → In Preparation → Ready → Out for Delivery → Delivered
        ├──► 3. Invoicing & WhatsApp: Generates the invoice/receipt and sends automated WhatsApp notifications or reminders
        ├──► 4. General Ledger & Cost Centers: Posts revenue, COGS, discounts, and VAT directly to the Chart of Accounts
        └──► 5. Live Dashboard: Updates Net Revenue, Cash Received, AR Aging, and Branch/Cost-Center P&L immediately
```

And for your team: **every payroll run posts its journal entry to the same books**, so salary and GOSI costs sit right alongside your sales and inventory margins.
